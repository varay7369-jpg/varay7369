All submitted material, likeness rights, and audition portfolios must be 100% authentic and owned by the applicant. Falsified records result in permanent disqualification.

Applicants and accepted candidates must maintain strict confidentiality regarding audition results, internal processes, and unpublished concepts.

VARAY fosters a zero-tolerance policy towards harassment, discrimination, or unsportsmanlike behavior across all online and offline spaces.
