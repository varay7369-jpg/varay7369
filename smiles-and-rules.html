CORE PHILOSOPHY & CODE OF ETHICS

SMILES AND RULES

The mutual pact between VARAY, our signed creators, and our global audience.

01. THE PHILOSOPHY OF SMILES

Art exists to elevate the human spirit. Every project under the VARAY banner is crafted to bring genuine resonance and inspiring energy to the world.

02. INTEGRITY & ZERO TOLERANCE

VARAY operates with absolute transparency. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against intellectual property plagiarism, unfair artist treatment, and deceptive practices.

03. ARTIST WELLBEING & GROWTH

Our training environment prioritizes mental health, continuous personal education, and sustainable creative schedules over short-term commercial pressure.

04. CONFIDENTIALITY & AUDITION PRIVACY

All audition recordings, personal metric data, and demo drafts submitted through our portal remain strictly confidential within the casting committee.
